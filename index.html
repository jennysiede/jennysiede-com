Jenny Siede is a technical apparel designer working at the intersection of design, materials, and production.

With a background in software engineering and manufacturing, she approaches garment design as a structured process. Her framework connects design decisions to sourcing, costing, construction, production, and the practical realities of the supply chain.

Her work begins before production orders are placed. She develops specifications, organizes product information, and builds the structure needed to reduce waste, protect quality, and keep production consistent.

Jenny is the founder of Green Neurons Design and Technologies PBC, where she works with creative and innovative founders, STEAM education programs, and nonprofits to turn complex ideas into clearer, sustainable, and cost-efficient operational systems.

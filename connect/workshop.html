Innovation Workshops

Beyond the product

Innovation in apparel is not limited to a new construction method, textile, or technology. It also means improving how products are conceived, developed, communicated, and manufactured. Jenny’s workshops use a systems-thinking approach to connect Product Lifecycle Management, technical design, sourcing, production, and AI-assisted workflows, helping participants see how each decision affects quality, waste, and the path from concept to production.

About Jenny

Jenny Siede is a technical apparel designer, workshop speaker, and industry juror working across apparel design, product development, manufacturing, and production systems.

She is a regular workshop speaker and juror across the apparel and textile industry — including Fashion Group International, Southern Methodist University, Texas Tech University, Wade College, Dallas College, 3F in Massachusetts, AIN, and The Fabric Show — and she serves as an Innovation Juror for the International Textile and Apparel Association (ITAA).

Her current work focuses on Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) and AI-assisted workflows that connect design decisions to technical development, sourcing, costing, production, and scalable operations.

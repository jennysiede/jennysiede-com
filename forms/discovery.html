Discovery Session

A free 15-minute conversation — Zoom or phone — to hear about your project and answer your questions. No pitch. No obligation.

What this is

The Discovery session is how every Green Neurons engagement begins. Before any contracts or commitments, we spend fifteen minutes getting oriented: what you are building, where you are in the process, and whether technical design consulting is actually the right fit for your project right now.

If it is not the right fit, we will tell you that too.

What we cover

  • What you are making and where you are in development
  • Key constraints — timeline, materials, production volume, budget range
  • Whether the project needs design, development, manufacturing support, or some combination
  • How Green Neurons works and what a formal engagement looks like

What to expect

Come prepared to describe your project in plain terms. You do not need a brief, a spec sheet, or a fully formed idea — just enough context for a real conversation. Jenny will ask direct questions and give direct answers.

The session runs fifteen minutes by design. If the conversation warrants more time, we schedule a follow-up.

What comes next

If our discovery call confirms mutual alignment, Green Neurons will issue a Letter of Intent — a plain-language pre-engagement document that establishes how we communicate, how each party’s work is protected, and what the path forward looks like. Signing it does not create a financial obligation.

When you are ready to proceed into formal engagement, a $500 deposit initiates the requirements analysis and Proforma development phase. Half of that deposit is credited toward your first Proforma invoice.

Want to read ahead? Download the Letter of Intent (PDF) — the document we send after the initial discovery session, once a potential partnership is confirmed.

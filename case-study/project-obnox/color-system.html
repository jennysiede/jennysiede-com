Project ObNOX™  ·  Color system

The Mocktails palette

Five signal colors. Each with a role, an emotional outcome, and a communication job. No color exists for aesthetic reasons.

"If a color cannot justify its existence, it should not be included."
#49FB5F ObNOX™ Green

Role: Signal color

Pattern interruption and curiosity generation. Engineered for attention, not comfort. Not corporate green. Not sustainability green. Not background green.

"What is this?"

Loud Signal Interrupt Unapologetic
#fb0643 Poison Lily™

Role: Provocateur

Introduce tension and challenge assumptions. Creates attraction and friction simultaneously — the cognitive dissonance is the point.

"Should I like this?"

Attraction Contrast Curiosity Friction
#e00fde Maniac Magenta™

Role: Disruptor

Break expectations and amplify creative energy. Playfulness and chaos as design intent, not accident.

"Who approved this?"

Playful Chaos Bold Experimental
#2af2dc Bermuda Harbor™

Role: Navigator

Provide balance, orientation, and visual breathing room within the system. The exhale after the interruption.

"Where do I go next?"

Calm Direction Clarity Focus
#fc7326 Crypto Tangerine™

Role: Catalyst

Create momentum and forward movement. The color that closes the loop — from curiosity to action.

Action Optimism Motion Opportunity

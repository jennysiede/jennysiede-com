The problem

How do you create a business card people actually pick up?

Most organizations use color to blend into their category. Safe palettes, muted tones, colors chosen because they look professional — not because they do anything.

The challenge for the Summer 2026 Green Neurons business card was different. The goal was not a card that looked appropriate. The goal was a card that got noticed, picked up, and remembered. That required a different question.

"The question is never: does this color look nice?
The question is: what emotional and cognitive response does this color create?"

The answer was ObNOX™ Green — a signal color engineered for attention, not comfort. What started as a single color decision evolved into a full experimental palette: the ObNOX™ Mocktails.

The approach

Color as a signal system, not decoration

Each color in the Mocktails palette was assigned a role, an emotional outcome, and a communication job. No color exists for aesthetic reasons. Every color performs a specific function in the attention sequence.

Project ObNOX™  ·  Artifacts

What got made

The first two ObNOX™ artifacts. Both started as a question about attention. Both answered it with color.

Artifact 01

Summer 2026 business card

Type Print — business card
Primary color ObNOX™ Green  #49FB5F
Treatment Full bleed signal color. Black typography. "gn" logomark scaled to edge. Three roles stacked vertically: designer, system, builder.
Intent The card that started the project. Designed to be noticed before it's read. If someone ignores it, the color failed.

"If someone ignores the card, the color failed."

Artifact 02

ObNOX™ Mocktails color study

Type Print — color reference card
Colors featured ObNOX™ Green · Poison Lily™ · Maniac Magenta™ · Bermuda Harbor™ · Crypto Tangerine™
Treatment White field with all five Mocktails expressed as typographic gestures, organic forms, and swatches. Each color performs its role within the composition.
Intent The color study card evolved from the business card exercise. A teaching artifact and signal system reference in one object.
